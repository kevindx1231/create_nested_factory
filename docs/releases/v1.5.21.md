## 更新日志

- 修复 Mekanism 指定类型提取和不同类型补取导致化学品丢失的问题，内外两侧保持资源守恒。
- 修复工厂内切换游戏模式后的飞行状态恢复；保留 NeoForge 飞行属性、外部飞行授权及旧存档兼容。

## Release notes

- Fixed chemical losses from wrong-type requests and mixed-type refills on both sides of Mekanism ports.
- Fixed flight restoration after game-mode changes while preserving NeoForge attributes, external flight grants and legacy saves.
- Reused logistics handlers, extension delegates and same-tick heat endpoints, with invalidation, unloading, binding and movement refreshes.
- Added chemical, heat and exploration regressions; existing regressions and the complete build passed.

Runtime resources, all 15 recipes and production plans are unchanged. This update has not received an in-game Mekanism/Pipez acceptance session.
